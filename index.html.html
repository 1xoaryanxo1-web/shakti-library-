Reserved vs. Unreserved Seats

Understanding the power of a dedicated personal workstation versus flexible seating.

Recommended

Reserved Seat Benefit

Dedicated Personal Workspace

  • Keep Books On Desk You can leave your books and notes permanently on your assigned desk (at your own security), saving daily packing hassle.
  • Guaranteed Permanent Spot No daily seat hunting. Your desk is locked for your shift every single day.
  • Higher Focus & Comfort Customize your study setup, lighting, and notebook placement for maximum productivity.
Reserve Dedicated Seat

Unreserved Seating

First-Come First-Serve Flexible Spots

  • Desk Must Be Cleared Daily You must take all books, bags, and personal study material with you at the end of every study session.
  • Variable Daily Seating Seats are assigned on arrival based on available vacancies for that specific shift.
  • Setup Adjustment Needed Requires setting up your reading materials and power connections every time you enter.
